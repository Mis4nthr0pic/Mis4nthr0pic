## How I think about applied AI

The model is the easy part. The work is everything around it:

- **Scoping.** Most teams build for an imagined user. The first job is finding the real one.
- **Human-in-the-loop placement.** Knowing where the model is unreliable and putting a human exactly there — not before, not after.
- **Evals before prompts.** If you can't measure regressions, your prompt engineering is just vibes.
- **Cost and latency as product constraints.** Tokens are a budget line.
- **Observability you can debug from at 2am**, not dashboards that look good in a review.
- **Deployment into infrastructure that wasn't designed for AI** — which is most infrastructure.

My projects are usually about these, not the model layer.
